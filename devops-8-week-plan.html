Do NOT set up your DevOps lab on the SAP work laptop

Short answer: No — it is not safe / not allowed for this.

  • It is a company device for SAP / work. Installing VirtualBox, Docker, Kubernetes tools, or random software can break policy and get you in trouble.
  • You may not have admin rights — and asking IT for them for private learning is usually the wrong path.
  • Mixing personal learning with company data (SAP, tickets, emails) is a security + GDPR risk in Germany.
  • If something goes wrong (malware, disk full, broken VPN), it can affect your job.

What to do instead: use a personal computer (laptop/desktop), or a cheap used laptop only for learning. Cloud free tiers (AWS/GitHub) for later weeks. Never put company SAP data into your learning projects.

Your study machine (safe options)

  • Best: Personal Windows/Mac/Linux laptop — install VirtualBox + Ubuntu here
  • Budget: Used laptop (~€150–250) dedicated to learning
  • Later weeks: Free cloud VMs (AWS free tier in Frankfurt) — still from personal account
  • Allowed on work laptop: only reading docs / watching videos / LinkedIn learning if company policy allows — no lab installs
